DETAILS THAT MATTER

The ground rules.In plain language.

An honest platform should be clear about how it works.

At 80% or higher, the claim becomes Verified and you can join the queue for that role. You get three attempts per skill; after using them, the claim is frozen for 48 hours.

Verified people join role queues. A squad is formed when all five roles are ready: Front-end, Back-end, UI/UX, QA, and Product Reviewer. A member cannot be in more than one active squad.

Each member is assessed separately using pull requests, code reviews, activity history, and submitted evidence. The contribution record is immutable once generated.

After more than 72 hours without activity, a member becomes Inactive. Their tasks may be paused or reassigned; the rest of the squad's evaluation remains independent.

No. It is not a job board, a job guarantee, a beginner LMS, an accredited certification system, a student marketplace, or a commercial software agency.
